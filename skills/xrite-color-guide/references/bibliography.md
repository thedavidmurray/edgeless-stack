## Key Standards Bodies

- **CIE (Commission Internationale de l’Éclairage)** — Established 1931 CIE XYZ color space and Standard Observer; foundational for all industrial color communication.
- **ICC (International Color Consortium)** — OS-independent device profile specification (ICC profiles).
- **ANSI Committee IT8** — Digital Data Exchange Standards; IT8 targets for scanner/printer characterization.
